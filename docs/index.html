02 — Introduction

The Decision Problem

A portfolio of hundreds of thousands of pages cannot be reviewed at uniform depth. This is a triage problem: given limited review capacity, which pages deserve human inspection first?

Not an automation problem — editorial judgment remains essential. A ranked review queue scored by a model identifies where human review is most likely to be productive.

DOES

  • Rank pages by observed search-visibility patterns
  • Produce a prioritized queue for human inspection
  • Provide decision-support with reason codes

DOES NOT

  • Prove causal impact of content changes
  • Predict Google's algorithm or ranking factors
  • Guarantee content-refresh success

Methodology

1

Single-month cross-section. No longitudinal or seasonal signal captured.
3
Page-Level Features Impressions + average search position
Two observable signals. No label-derived, future, or product-decision features.
4
Transparent Baseline Rule-based position + impression scoring
What a human analyst might build without ML. Fixed thresholds, fully interpretable.
5
Logistic Regression Primary model — calibrated probability scores
scikit-learn LogisticRegression, max_iter=1000, StandardScaler fitted on training fold only.
6
5-Fold Grouped Validation GroupKFold by client — zero client overlap
Each fold: ~44 clients train, ~11 test. Tests generalization to unseen clients, not just unseen pages.
7
Ranked Review Queue Top 1,000 pages for human inspection
The final output: an ordered list scored by LR probability, ready for human triage.
GroupKFold: Grouped by client_hash_id (55 clients). Each fold holds out ~11 clients entirely. No client appears in both train and test. This tests generalization to unseen clients.

Label Definition

opportunity_proxy = (impressions > 0) & (clicks == 0). A page is proxy-positive when it has impressions but zero clicks. This is a proxy, not a future prediction target. Base rate: 32.6%.

Baseline & Models

Baseline: Rule-based scoring combining position and impression thresholds. Logistic Regression (primary): max_iter=1000, random_state=42, features standardized via StandardScaler (training fold only). Random Forest (secondary): 100 estimators, same GroupKFold structure.

06 — Limitations

What This Study Does Not Claim

Limitations are presented prominently because honest framing makes the result trustworthy.

  1. Proxy & Label Construction The target (opportunity_proxy) is constructed from the same March 2026 window as features. impressions > 0 appears in both the feature set and the label definition, creating a structural relationship. Precision@K is partly influenced by this overlap. This is not a clean future prediction target.
  2. Generalization & Client Concentration GroupKFold with 55 clients tests across groups within one portfolio; results may not generalize to different industries or search landscapes. One client dominates ~50% of the top-1000 queue. Random Forest was evaluated in Week 5 but not independently audited in Week 6 and not used for the Week 7 action queue.
  3. Observational & Non-Causal All associations are observed in search data only. No causality is established. The output is a ranked review queue, not prescribed actions; human judgment remains essential for every editorial decision.
  4. Single Window & Production Scope Only March 2026 data — no seasonal, trend, or longitudinal patterns captured. Production deployment requires accounting for client concentration and ongoing signal monitoring. Results are directional evidence for similar triage tasks, not general-purpose SEO prediction.

Recommendations

  1. 01
    Start with the top-ranked pages The 1,000-page REVIEW tier. ~971 proxy-positive under evaluation — pages with impressions but no clicks.
    Signal: impressions present, no clicks → Action: content/metadata review → Rationale: highest probability of productive human intervention
  2. 02
    Use reason codes and archetypes Each page has a reason code and behavioral archetype providing starting context for the human reviewer.
    Signal: position + impression combination → Action: archetype-guided investigation → Rationale: different patterns require different review approaches
  3. 03
    Monitor the middle tier 174,304 MONITOR pages have observable signal. Track for changes that might elevate priority.
    Signal: impressions > 0 but below REVIEW threshold → Action: periodic reassessment → Rationale: position shifts may surface new opportunities
  4. 04
    Maintain human judgment The model scores where to look; the human decides what to do. Publishing, rewriting, merging, pruning all require editorial judgment.
    Signal: model output is a rank, not a decision → Action: human review as final step → Rationale: editorial context cannot be automated
